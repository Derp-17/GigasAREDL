Submission guidelines

Please follow these rules when submitting challenge completions or verifications. Records that do not meet these requirements may be rejected by the moderation team.

Completion & verification evidence

  • Maximum 240 FPS. Completions and verifications may be played and recorded at up to 240 frames per second.
  • Audible clicks required. Video evidence must include clearly audible input clicks.
  • No cheats. Cheating, hacked completions, macros and other illegitimate assistance are not allowed.
  • Video evidence required. Submit a working link to your completion or verification video.

Challenge requirements

  • Maximum length: 30 seconds. Listed levels must be no longer than 30 seconds.
  • Maximum click rate: 11 CPS. Challenges must not require more than 11 clicks per second.
  • All submitted challenges and records are subject to staff review.
Before submitting: Check that your evidence link is accessible, your clicks can be heard, and your run follows the rules above. Staff review submissions before they count toward player profiles.
