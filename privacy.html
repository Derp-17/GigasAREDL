Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Giga's Challenge List is a community-run Geometry Dash challenge-ranking website. This page describes how the site currently handles information and how advertising may work in future.

Information you provide

When you sign in, the authentication provider may process your email address or Discord sign-in information. If you create a player profile, the website stores your chosen username, optional bio, and profile-related timestamps. Support tickets contain your chosen category, subject, message, staff replies and ticket status. When you submit a completion, it stores the challenge, percentage, evidence-video link, optional notes, review status, and moderation information. Public profiles, approved records and leaderboard results may be visible to visitors.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts, manage challenge rankings, verify submitted completions, moderate records, prevent abuse, and display public player profiles and leaderboards. Authorised staff can review registered player profiles and submissions. Account emails and authentication tokens are not displayed in the public player directory.

Services involved

The site uses Supabase for account authentication, Cloudflare for website hosting and application/database services, GitHub for source-code hosting, and may display videos or thumbnails from YouTube. These services may process technical information under their own policies when you interact with them.

Cookies and local storage

Account sign-in and site preferences use browser storage necessary to provide features you request. A local choice is also stored when you accept or reject optional advertising storage; you can change this choice using the Cookie preferences button in the footer. Rejecting optional storage does not prevent you from using the challenge list. Browser storage can also be cleared through your browser settings.

Advertising

The website has labelled advertisement placeholders and includes Google AdSense site-verification code while the site is under review. Paid advertising placements are not yet activated. Before serving ads, the operator must configure an appropriate consent-management platform, including a Google-certified CMP where required, and update this policy to describe the actual advertising technologies, purposes, partners and choices. Google may process technical data when its verification script loads. See Google's advertising privacy information.

Retention and your choices

Account and submission records are generally kept while needed for site operation, rankings and moderation; a precise retention schedule has not yet been finalised. You can edit your player profile through the account page. You can download a JSON export of your profile, submissions, approved records and support tickets from Settings. Non-staff users can also permanently delete their accounts there after explicit confirmation. This removes their Supabase sign-in identity and live player profile, submissions, approved records and support tickets; their leaderboard entries disappear. Staff accounts require assistance from the site owner. A separate support-based deletion request remains available. Provider backups, operational logs and retained moderation history may persist according to applicable retention requirements. For other privacy requests, use the Support Centre while signed in.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features, providers or advertising arrangements change. The updated date will be revised.
